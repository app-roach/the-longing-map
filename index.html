    Editing layout

    Select a room on the map to see where it leads.

    Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Right-click anywhere to drop a marker, or pick one from the toolbar on the left.

    Rooms you haven't visited stay hidden. Mark a room as visited (V or right-click) to reveal the way on, or turn off Hide unvisited in the Map tab to see the whole map.

          Your data

          Markers, visited rooms and notes are saved in this browser. Export a copy to back them up or move them to another computer.

          An interactive map of every cave room in The Longing, the 400-day adventure and idle game by Studio Seufz (2020). The Shade waits deep under the earth for the King to wake, and this map lays the whole underground out in one place.

          Search for a room or click one to see where its passages, doorways and dark climbs lead. Mark rooms as visited, drop markers and notes as you explore, and move rooms around to untangle the map. Everything you add stays in your browser.
